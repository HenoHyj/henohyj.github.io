A real sensor problem

Compared an LSTM with a Latent ODE using NOAA observations from Scripps Pier. The task forecasts six environmental channels over 24 hours using 48 hours of history. The evaluation covers random dropout, block outages, and native irregular sampling.

What the comparison showed

On a clean regular grid, the LSTM performed better. Under increasingly irregular sampling, the Latent ODE’s relative skill stayed near 0.62 while the LSTM degraded from 0.48 toward 0.78. A tidal-clock ablation helped separate architectural robustness from the additional accuracy provided by periodic features.

Results & scope

ECE 228 team project with Yifan Peng, Zihao Yang, and Zixuan Chen. Results depend on the missing-data regime.
